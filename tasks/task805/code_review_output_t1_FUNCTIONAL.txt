## Summary

Of the 10 reported findings, 2 were genuine bugs and have been fixed; the other 8 were false positives (confirmed by reading the actual code):

**Fixed:**
1. **`TornarControlesVisiveis`** ÔÇö had a typo (`loc_oControl` instead of `loc_oControle`) plus a call to `THIS.TornarControlesVisiveis(loc_oControl)`, a procedure with no `LPARAMETERS`.
