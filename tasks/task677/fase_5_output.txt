## The real defect this uncovered (measured in VFP9)

The itens grid is the data area, and it was silently destroyed on every list change. `BuscarItensDaLista` does `USE IN` + `SQLEXEC` on the alias **while the grid is still bound to it**. I reproduced the damage with a probe:

```
[4] bind inicial:          C1.CC=[CHECK1] C1.Sparse=.F. C1.W=22 C3.W=290 C3.hdr=[Descri├º├úo do Produto] C4.mask=[999,999,999.99] Check1existe=.T.
[5] cursor RECRIADO:       C1.CC=[Text1]  C1.Sparse=.T. C1.W=7  C3.W=28  C3.hdr=[Dpros]                C4.mask=[]              Check1existe=.F.
[6] apos o contrato:       C1.CC=[CHECK1] C1.Sparse=.F. C1.W=22 C3.W=290 C3.hdr=[Descri├º├úo do Produto] C4.mask=[999,999,999.99] Check1existe=.T.
```

From the **second list selected onward** the grid showed raw column names (`Dpros`), 7px columns, no masks, and **no checkbox** ÔÇö so nothing could be marked, which is the whole purpose of the dialog. No error, no log.

The non-obvious part: reapplying properties is not enough. `Check1existe` goes `.T. ÔåÆ .F.` ÔÇö the cursor recreation rebuilds the `Column` objects and **destroys the AddObject'd CheckBox**. My first contract version restored widths/headers/masks but left `CurrentControl=Text1`; `GarantirCheckBoxItens()` re-creates the control and re-binds its 5 handlers (`When` + the 4 Pattern #185 toggle handlers), guarded so bindings happen once per instance.
