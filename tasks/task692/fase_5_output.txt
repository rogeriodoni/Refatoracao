## Two things I measured instead of assuming

**`TabIndex` is a clamped 1..N ordinal, not a free integer.** My first pass transcribed the dump's 3/18/24 literally; the probe read back 1/2/3. Measured on a synthetic 3-child form:

- no assignment at all ÔåÆ 1/2/3 (`AddObject` already numbers by creation order)
- ascending 3ÔåÆ18ÔåÆ24 ÔåÆ 1/2/3 ÔÇö identical, a no-op
- **descending 24ÔåÆ18ÔåÆ3 ÔåÆ grid and button swap** (grd=3, cmg=2)

The dump's 18/24 are stale Form Designer ordinals; the legacy clamps them the same way, so the relative order (header ÔåÆ grid ÔåÆ button) matches.
