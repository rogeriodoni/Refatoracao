**Summary of what I did and why it deviates from the generic template:**

The Fase 4 prompt's generic instructions (ConfigurarPaginaLista + 6 CRUD buttons Incluir/Visualizar/Alterar/Excluir/Buscar/Encerrar + AlternarPagina) assume a CRUD form with a PageFrame. I verified against the legacy dump (`SigPrGst_form_codigo_fonte.txt`) that **SIGPRGST has none of that**: zero PageFrame, and its only two buttons are `CmdGrava` ("Confirmar") and `CmdCancela` ("Sair"). This is a known recurring pattern (documented in memory as the "gate exige CRUD/PageFrame inexistente" family) where the generic phase template doesn't fit OPERACIONAL dispatcher forms ÔÇö building the CRUD surface anyway would invent UI that doesn't exist, violating PILAR 1.

Instead I implemented the real Fase 4 surface already sketched in this form's own Fase-3 roadmap comment:
- `ConfigurarGrids()`: creates `grd_4c_GrdCab` (pedidos pendentes, 7 columns transcribed from the dump ÔÇö Emp/Grupo Origem/Conta Origem/Conta Destino/Grupo Destino/Confirma├º├úo/Movimenta├º├úo) and `grd_4c_GrdIte` (itens do pedido, 7 columns ÔÇö Produto/Descri├º├úo/M/Pr.Unit./Quantidade/Total), binding `RecordSource`/`ControlSource` to `csCabec`/`csItens` only when those shared-session cursors already exist (guards against CLAUDE.md regra #41 crash), with headers/widths reapplied after the bind per the "Grid perde cabe├ºalhos" rule.
- `GrdCabAfterRowColChange`: transcribed from the legacy grid event, re-scoping `csItens` and refreshing `grd_4c_GrdIte` when the user picks a different pedido row.
- `ConfigurarBotoesAcao()`: the real `shp_4c_ShpP2` decorative shape plus `cmd_4c_CmdGrava`/`cmd_4c_CmdCancela`, with exact legacy positions/icons/captions and the standalone-button `Themes=.T.`+`DisabledPicture` pattern.
