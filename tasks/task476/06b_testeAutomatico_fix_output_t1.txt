Fixed. The `opt_4c_PagaComiss` OptionGroup was missing `ButtonCount = 2` before the WITH block, causing VFP9 to fail when accessing `.Buttons(2)` since a dynamically-added OptionGroup may not default to 2 buttons. Added `loc_oPg2.opt_4c_PagaComiss.ButtonCount = 2` immediately after `AddObject`, following the same pattern already correctly used for `opt_4c_Classif` (which had `ButtonCount = 3`).
