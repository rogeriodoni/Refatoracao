## Summary

**Root cause found and confirmed empirically** (via VFP9 probe scripts, not just static analysis): `txt_4c_Digitos` is the first enabled/tab-stop control on the form (`txt_4c_Valor` is `ReadOnly`+`TabStop=.F.`, `txt_4c_Cartao` starts `Enabled=.F.`). When the form becomes active after `Show()`, VFP9 automatically gives it focus, firing `DigitosGotFocus` ÔåÆ `DigitosGotFocusExec`. In test mode there's no real SiTef terminal, so `ContinuarSiTef()` returns `-1` ÔåÆ `ProcessarErroTef` ÔåÆ `GravarRetornoFalha` ÔåÆ `THIS.EncerrarDialogo()` ÔåÆ `Hide()`, making the form invisible *before* the test harness can read `.Visible` ÔÇö with no exception anywhere, exactly matching "Formulario nao ficou visivel" / Return Code 2.

I verified this by instantiating the form headlessly with `gb_4c_ModoTeste=.T.`: forcing focus onto `txt_4c_Digitos` flipped `Visible` from `.T.` to `.F.` and populated `this_cRetornoTef` ÔÇö proof the hide chain fires. The two previous fix attempts (directory guard for `C:\client\Resp`, and `ShowWindow`/`WindowType` defaults) were already correct but didn't address this.

**Fix**: added a test-mode guard at the top of `DigitosGotFocusExec()` in `Formsigoptef.prg` (same pattern already used by `ValidarParametros()`/`CarregarParametrosPagamento()` in the same file) that returns early when `gb_4c_ModoTeste`/`gb_4c_ValidandoUI` is active, before touching the SiTef protocol. Re-tested: with the guard in place, forcing focus onto the field no longer hides the form (`Visible` stays `.T.`, `this_cRetornoTef` stays empty). Production behavior is unchanged ÔÇö the guard only short-circuits in test/UI-validation mode.

Both `Formsigoptef.prg` and `sigoptefBO.prg` still compile cleanly. No business logic, fields, or lookups were touched.
