The phase prompt's generic objective ("ConfigurarPaginaLista() com Grid, 6 bot├Áes CRUD, AlternarPagina()") is the standard template for `frmcadastro`-style forms, but `SIGOPCHI` is explicitly flagged as an OPERACIONAL consulta form with its own custom legacy layout (Page1=Filtro, Page2=Hist├│rico) ÔÇö there's no Lista/Dados CRUD structure at all. Per CLAUDE.md's rule against inventing fake CRUD functionality, I followed the actual legacy source instead:
