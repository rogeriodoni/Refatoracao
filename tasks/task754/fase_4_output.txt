## The trap in this form

The legacy **renames four Columns**, and the dump reports it two ways that disagree: the grid property block is keyed by **SCX position**, while the `Header1`/`Text1` blocks and the Init binding are keyed by **name** ÔÇö position 6ÔåÆ`Column7`, 7ÔåÆ`Column8`, 8ÔåÆ`Column9`, 9ÔåÆ`Column6`. Reading both by the same key would have scrambled header, width, mask and data column on four columns, and the screen would have opened with **no error at all**, just data in the wrong columns. The mapping is confirmed semantically: header Ôåö ControlSource agrees on all 12. Also, the `ControlSource` values stored in the SCX are **stale** (it puts `dbetiqueta.pesos` on the "Pre├ºo De" column); the Init rebinds all 12, so that is the source I transcribed.
