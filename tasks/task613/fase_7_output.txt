Adding fake CRUD stubs would have violated the project's "no stubs" rule and the established convention (mirrored exactly in the sibling `FormSigPrGf1.prg`: `BtnProcessarClick`/`BtnEncerrarClick`). Instead I implemented the actual remaining work flagged by the previous phases' own comments:

- **`PopularComboChaves()`** ÔÇö populates the "Grupo / Vendedor" combo from the BO's distinct-keys cursor and repositions/resizes `cnt_4c_Grf2`, transcribed from the legacy `If Empty(ListCount)` block.
- **`MGeraGrafico()`** ÔÇö single source of truth (equivalent to legacy `mgeragrafico`), called both by `ExecutarCargaInicial()` (Init-time) and `CboChave1Click()` (combo click), removing the duplicated logic that was inline in `CboChave1Click`.
- **`DesenharGrafico()`** ÔÇö renders the MSGraph.Chart in the OLE control from the BO's computed series, with its own local OLE-binary cache cursor (`cursor_4c_OleGrafico1`).
- **`BtnGraficoClick`** / **`BtnEncerrarClick`** ÔÇö wired via `BINDEVENT` to the CommandGroup's two buttons, transcribing `cmdImprimir.Click`/`cmdSair.Click`.
