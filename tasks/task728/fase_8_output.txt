The form arrived from Phase 7 nearly complete, so I audited rather than assumed. Two real defects surfaced, both now fixed:

**1. The legacy `PROCEDURE Release` was only ┬╝ reproduced.** The dump does four things ÔÇö clear `Grade.RecordSource`, clear two `Column.ControlSource`, then `Use In LocalEmb`. `Destroy` only did the last one, closing the alias while the Grid still pointed at it.
