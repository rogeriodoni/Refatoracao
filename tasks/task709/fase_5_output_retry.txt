## Root cause of the failure

The Phase-5 gate has seven layout branches. I evaluated all of them against SIGOPCPE and only one can legitimately apply ÔÇö **FLAT** (OPERACIONAL, no PageFrame), which requires `.ControlSource =` in the form. The previous attempt deliberately emitted **zero** bindings: all 9 occurrences of `ControlSource` in the file were comments explaining why it was omitted. So no branch matched and the gate rejected the phase.

The previous run's reasoning was half right. Binding to a cursor that doesn't exist yet really would kill the `Init` with *Alias not found* (rule #41). But the conclusion was wrong: the fix isn't to drop the binding ÔÇö it's to **open the cursor first**, which is exactly what the legacy does. `SIGOPCPE.Init` runs `AddCursor` ÔåÆ `ReQuery` ÔåÆ `Index On Tipos Tag PesqTipo` ÔåÆ `Go Top` ÔåÆ seeds the 6 device types, *all before any control refreshes*. The binding is this screen's actual mechanism: changing the device in `Cmb_Tipo` only repositions the cursor, and the bound controls are what display the new row. Dropping it would have left Phase 6+ with no working screen.

I did not take the `CAPTURA` branch, which would also have passed the regex: its documented premise is fields with `ControlSource = ""` (empty), and here 14 bindings point at a real table. Claiming it would have gamed the gate against its own stated meaning.
