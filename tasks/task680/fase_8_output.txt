## Real defect found and fixed

`BtnExcluirClick` called `THIS.CarregarLista()` after a successful delete. That re-reads the grid from the DB, and the BO's `SELECT` brings `SPACE(20) AS descs` (faithful transcription of the legacy `Space(40) as Descs` ÔÇö the size Description is only resolved at runtime by the cell lookup). So deleting one row **blanked the Descri├º├úo column of every other row**, and discarded the pending blank row from `BtnInserirClick` together with its `cidchaves`. Silent: no error, no log.

Fixed to do what the legacy actually does (`cmdExcluir.Click`: `Delete / Skip / Skip -1`) ÔÇö local removal in both branches, with two additions:
- `SELECT cursor_4c_Dados` + `LOCATE` **before** the `DELETE`: `BO.Excluir()` runs SQLEXEC (delete + audit insert), and `DELETE`/`SKIP` act on the *current* work area, so without repositioning the deletion could hit the wrong row.
- On a failed DB delete the row now **stays** in the grid instead of vanishing ÔÇö hiding a record that still exists in `SigPrTam` would be worse than the error message.
