            Not IsEmpty(crSigOpFp.Agencias) And Not IsEmpty(crSigOpFp.NContas)

That `If` is exactly what `SigOpNchBO.CarregarFormasPagtoCheque` reproduces as SQL:
```sql

  AND emichqs = 1
  AND RTRIM(bancos) <> ''
  AND RTRIM(agencias) <> ''
  AND RTRIM(ncontas) <> ''
```
